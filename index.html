Our Story

A house built on
obsessive curation

Maison Noir was founded in 2019 in the belief that true luxury is not about abundance — it is about the precise selection of the few. We exist to help you live with less, but better.

Every product in our catalogue passes a rigorous selection process. We visit makers personally, scrutinise the provenance of every material, and refuse to list anything we would not own ourselves. If it fails to move us, it doesn't make the cut.

We work with independent ateliers across Japan, Scandinavia, Italy, and the British Isles — small workshops where generations of skill are poured into every object. We believe the maker's story is inseparable from the product itself.

Our catalogue is intentionally small. Five objects at a time, rotated seasonally. We would rather show you five extraordinary things than five hundred mediocre ones.

Maison Noir ships globally, with care. Every order is hand-wrapped in archival tissue, sealed with our wax stamp, and shipped in a bespoke rigid box. Unboxing should feel like an occasion.
